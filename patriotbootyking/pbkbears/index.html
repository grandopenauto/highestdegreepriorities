First-drop FAQ

Enough detail to understand the concept without locking the product before the physical prototype tells us what looks best.

Are the cards being printed yet?

Not yet. The current art is a prototype direction. The next production step is ordering physical samples and comparing card stock, foil, sparkle and finishing options.

Does every card have a QR code?

No — that is part of what makes the unlock cards special. The base set can stay purely collectible while selected chase cards connect to songs and other digital content.

Will there be numbered cards?

The concept supports serial-numbered parallels such as /100, /25, /10 and 1/1. Exact print runs can be finalized after presale demand and prototype testing.

What happens when I scan an unlock card?

The card can open a PBK-hosted mobile page containing the bear's song, story, video, card information or future collector content.

Can the digital content change later?

Yes. The printed QR can point to a permanent PBK URL while the content behind that URL evolves over time.

Is this already a paid preorder?

No. This version captures presale interest only. A paid checkout can be connected after product specs, pricing and fulfillment terms are finalized.
