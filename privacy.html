Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026.

Highest Degree Priorities respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information submitted through our website, forms, assessments, and SMS communications.

Information We Collect

We may collect your name, email address, mobile phone number, business information, assessment responses, service interests, consent records, and messages you send to us.

How We Use Information

We use information to respond to inquiries, provide requested services, coordinate consultations and appointments, deliver customer support, operate lead and follow-up systems, and provide information about Highest Degree Priorities services when you have consented to receive it.

SMS Communications

SMS communications are optional. Our SMS Opt-In Form collects informational/service-related SMS consent separately from promotional/marketing SMS consent.

To receive informational and conversational SMS messages, a user must affirmatively select an unchecked checkbox agreeing to recurring messages from Highest Degree Priorities about inquiries, requested services, appointments, customer service, and related follow-up. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

Promotional and marketing SMS consent is collected through a second, distinct, unchecked, and optional checkbox. A user may separately choose to receive occasional promotional or marketing messages about Highest Degree Priorities services, offers, and business solutions. Promotional/marketing consent is not required to receive informational or service-related messages and is not a condition of purchase.

We retain information about the source of the opt-in, the applicable consent scope selected by the user, and the consent disclosure presented at the time of submission to support our messaging compliance records.

Mobile Information and SMS Consent

Mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in data, and SMS consent will not be shared, sold, rented, or otherwise disclosed to third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

We may use service providers that process information solely as needed to operate our systems or deliver communications on our behalf. Those providers are not permitted to use your mobile information or SMS consent for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

Other Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited non-SMS information with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate our business, process payments, provide requested services, protect our rights, or comply with applicable law.

Your Choices

You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. For SMS help, reply HELP or contact us at contact@highestdegreepriorities.com. You may also contact us to ask questions about your information or our privacy practices.

Contact

Questions? Contact Highest Degree Priorities at contact@highestdegreepriorities.com.
