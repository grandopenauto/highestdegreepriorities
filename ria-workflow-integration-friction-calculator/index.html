Transparent method

How the score works.

The score is a prioritization aid built from the conditions you enter. It deliberately avoids unsupported industry benchmarks.

25 points

Systems

0 points at one system, rising linearly to 25 points at six or more systems touched.

20 points

Handoffs

4 points per manual handoff, capped at five handoffs.

20 points

Re-keying

5 points per duplicate-entry step, capped at four steps.

20 points

Exceptions

Exception rate contributes linearly from 0 to 20 points, capped at a 30% exception rate.

15 points

Ownership

Clear owner: 0. Shared: 7.5. Unclear or case-dependent: 15.

Human judgment is not scored as friction.

If judgment is critical, the calculator adds an operating boundary: automate preparation, routing, reconciliation and evidence where appropriate, while keeping professional review/approval with the responsible person.

Interpretation

Use the score to choose where to investigate—not to justify automation by itself.

0–24 · Low

Relatively simple structure. Improve only where actual delays, errors or cost justify it.

25–49 · Moderate

Some integration friction. Inspect the dominant factor before adding another system.

50–74 · High

Multiple structural burdens are interacting. Map the workflow, ownership and exception path before automating more.

75–100 · Very high

The workflow is structurally complex. Treat architecture and operating design as the first problem, not tool selection.
