AI receptionist pricing FAQ

The main billing questions before launch.

What does setup cover?

Setup covers configuration, campaign scripting, dashboard and lead-pipeline setup, testing, and preparing the initial AI receptionist flow for launch.

Why separate usage credits?

Voice AI, phone minutes, and processing have variable costs. Prepaid credits keep those costs transparent instead of hiding them inside an unlimited-usage promise.

What happens when credits run low?

The account can be topped up with additional credit, and campaign usage can be paused or limited to avoid unexpected overages.
