  • Pickup and delivery scheduling

  • Status, exception and follow-up tracking

Operational Scope

Build the workflow around the carrier.

Load Workflow

Organize potential loads, carrier requirements, decisions, confirmations and next actions in a visible pipeline.

Communication

Structure broker contacts, load questions, appointment coordination, updates and follow-up so important details do not disappear.

Documents

Coordinate rate confirmations, BOL/POD handoffs and downstream invoicing or bookkeeping inputs.

Exception Management

Surface delays, missing documents, appointment problems and unresolved tasks instead of letting them become silent failures.

Operating Visibility

Create a clearer view of active loads, completed work, unresolved items and the next operational constraint.

Automation Layer

Add email, voice, reminders, task routing or custom workflow automation where it actually reduces repetitive coordination.

Founding Carrier Model

Flat monthly dispatch options can reduce the percentage-of-revenue penalty as a carrier grows.

HDP is developing flat monthly managed-service options alongside scoped projects. Early pricing is determined by equipment type, operating region, load volume, workflow depth and the amount of human coordination required.

A good fit may include:

  • Owner-operators who want organized carrier-side dispatch support
  • Small fleets needing shared operating visibility
  • Carriers with inconsistent document or follow-up processes
  • Businesses that want dispatch connected to accounting, recruiting or workflow automation

Important Scope Boundary

Carrier-side dispatch support is not the same thing as freight brokerage.

HDP structures dispatch services around preexisting carrier relationships and carrier-directed workflows. Shipper research, SEO and sales intelligence are offered separately. HDP does not represent itself as accepting freight and allocating it among unrelated carriers under this service.
