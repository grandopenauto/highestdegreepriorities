Recruiting workflow

  • Attract and capture candidates
  • Collect role and qualification information
  • Screen and route applicants
  • Follow up and schedule interviews
  • Track qualified, approved and hired candidates

The goal is not simply more applicants. It is a more reliable path from interest to a seated driver or owner-operator relationship.

Recruiting Operating System

Candidate interest → screening → qualification → interview → onboarding.
